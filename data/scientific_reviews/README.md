# Scientific reviews

Record the method and outcome of each evidence appraisal.

Use GRADE to assess certainty of a body of evidence and strength of a recommendation. Use PRISMA 2020 to report systematic reviews; PRISMA is not an evidence-quality score. Select risk-of-bias tools appropriate to study design.

Record search strategy, databases, search date, inclusion/exclusion criteria, study designs, risk of bias, consistency, indirectness, imprecision, publication bias, certainty, applicability and reviewer decision.

Reviews are claim- and context-specific. Conflicts and uncertainty remain visible. Human review is required before activation of clinically consequential claims or rules.
